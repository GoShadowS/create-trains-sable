# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone who participates in this project, whether as a player, issue reporter,
contributor or maintainer, should be able to do so without harassment. We pledge
to keep the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and any future community
space welcoming to people of any experience level, background, identity or
opinion.

## Expected behaviour

- Be polite and assume the other person is trying to help.
- Give technical detail instead of judgements about people.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change and explain why.
- Respect the privacy of others; do not publish private messages or personal
  information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, discriminatory language, threats or sexualised content.
- Repeated personal attacks, trolling, or deliberate disruption of a discussion.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their consent.
- Submitting malicious code, misleading crash reports, or files intended to harm
  other contributors' machines.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests,
discussions, commits, review comments and any official chat or social account
linked from the README. It also applies when someone represents the project in
public.

## Reporting

Open an issue at https://github.com/GoShadowS/create-trains-sable/issues with the
label `conduct` and describe what happened, where, and which messages or commits
are involved. If the report names a private individual or contains sensitive
information, use the private Security Advisory form at
https://github.com/GoShadowS/create-trains-sable/security/advisories/new instead
and say in the title that it is a conduct report.

There is no dedicated email address. Reports are visible to repository
maintainers; they will acknowledge the report and keep the discussion with the
reporter private where the platform allows it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, block accounts, or ban a
person from the repository. The response depends on the impact and on whether
the behaviour is repeated:

1. A private warning and a request to edit the offending message.
2. A temporary block from issues and pull requests.
3. A permanent ban for severe or repeated violations.

Maintainers who enforce these rules are expected to act consistently and to
explain their decision to the person affected. A maintainer involved in a report
should ask another maintainer to handle it.
